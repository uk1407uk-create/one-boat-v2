# ONE BOAT Probability Engine (OBPE) v1.0

- Core: scenario prediction × normalized trifecta probability distribution × market evaluation gap × uncertainty.
- Old 100-point score gate is not used for purchase decisions.
- All 120 trifecta combinations are normalized to sum to 1.
- Market probability is derived from normalized inverse odds for comparison only.
- Conservative probability applies an uncertainty haircut.
- Current odds are used with a 5% adverse odds buffer; no unsupported closing-odds forecast is claimed.
- Bet count is dynamic.
- Stakes are optimized jointly because trifecta outcomes within one race are mutually exclusive.
- Unit: JPY 100.
- Hard maximum stake per race: JPY 5,000.
- Recalculate around 10 minutes before close; final decision within 5 minutes.
- Model version: OBPE-1.0.0.
- Probability version: scenario-pl-v1.
- Calibration: empirical-shrinkage-v1; performance calibration remains subject to continuing validation.
